REMaster runs a 10-person company (engineering, design, growth, marketing, sales, customer success, QA) for a whole quarter. It remembers the small things for months and checks its own forgetting.

1 · The problem

Long-horizon agents forget the wrong things

An agent that works for weeks piles up history. Today there are only two options, and both fail:

Keep everything context rot

The context grows every day. Cost and latency climb, and the model drowns in its own history until it stops making sense.

Summarize silent loss

A generic model guesses today what will matter later. It doesn't know the plan, so it deletes the wrong details, permanently. Nobody checks.

For a manager, the facts that matter most are tiny and old, and those are exactly what a summary drops:

"Sarah is off in week 9 (wedding)"
"Tom underestimates by ~40%"
"Lina is 2× faster on backend"
"Client moved the beta to week 10"
WEEK 2
Sarah mentions her wedding at standup.
WEEK 3
"Summarize week-2 standups" → the line is gone.
WEEK 9
Launch work assigned to Sarah. She's away. The plan breaks.
Our claim: REMaster tests its own forgetting, and when a lost fact would change a decision, it restores the exact fact.

2 · The architecture

A nightly memory loop: work, sleep, check, recover

Two roles: a long-horizon Strategist owns the quarter plan in an explicit, editable State Doc. A Doer runs each day. Every night the Doer's memory goes through this loop:

1☀️ Work

Doer · daytime

Runs standups, assigns work, unblocks people.

Works from a small working memory, plus memory_sql: SQL over its entire past.

→

2🌙 Sleep

Disposable fork of the Strategist · night

A copy of the Strategist, which knows the quarter plan, reviews the day and curates the Doer's memory by intent:

keepfoldpromotearchive

Then the fork is thrown away, so the Strategist is never polluted by cleanup work.

↑
↓

4♻️ Recover

Judge · on divergence

A judge separates noise from decision-changing loss, and bisects which archived fact mattered.

The exact fact is restored from the append-only log. No re-summarizing.

←

3👥 Check

Shadow Doers · read-only

Shadows with lagged memory (1 day, 1 week, never cleaned) replay key decisions.

If the real Doer and a shadow decide differently, the cleanup lost something useful: divergence = harmful forgetting.

🗄️
Nothing is ever deleted.

Every event from every agent goes to an append-only log (Tinybird Rawtree). Every "forget" is only a pointer, e.g. [archived #E1190: Sarah availability, wk 2 → memory_sql].

Same-day policies (during the day, not overnight)

Nobody idles

When a ticket gets blocked ("our provider got delayed, we'll only have it tomorrow"), the manager blocks the right ticket and immediately moves the person onto other work that fits their skills.

Staffing policy

Work is assigned by skill, availability and learned pace (e.g. Tom's estimates are padded by 40%). Nobody gets launch work during a known absence.

3 · Why this solves long-term memory

Forgetting that is intentional, tested and reversible

Intentional

The curator is a fork of the Strategist, so it knows the quarter plan. It archives "wedding in week 9" during week 3 because it isn't relevant yet, not because it looks unimportant.

Tested

Every forgetting decision is checked against future behavior. Lagged shadows show whether the cleaned memory would still make the same call.

Reversible

The archive is append-only and queryable with SQL. A wrong "forget" is undone by restoring the exact original fact.
