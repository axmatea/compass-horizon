Privacy.

COMPASS by AX Media Global.
Updated 14 November 2026.

What we receive

Account details, sign-in identifiers and optional profile preferences. With Google sign-in, this includes your name, email address, profile picture and Google account identifier. Email accounts also store a password hash.

We process the prompts and edits you submit. Account features can store your projects, revisions, uploaded images and application records. Session information, IP addresses, browser details and usage records support sign-in, security and operation of the service.

How it is used

To sign you in, protect your account, generate and refine projects, save your work and provide account recovery. Google sign-in data identifies and links your COMPASS account; it is not included in AI model requests. Google sign-in does not request access to Gmail or Google Drive.

Service providers

Railway hosts COMPASS and its configured account storage and backups. Google handles Google sign-in. When email delivery is enabled, Resend receives your email address and the verification or recovery message.

Generation requests send your submitted text and relevant project context to the configured AI provider: Anthropic, General Compute or Nebius. These providers process requests under their own policies. Application records and account credentials are not part of the application-generation context.

Storage and sharing

Sign-in uses essential session cookies. Drafts and device saves use browser storage. When accounts are enabled, saved account content is held in private server storage; configured encrypted backups contain copies.

Publishing a website creates a public copy of its selected page. Private application records and account details are not published with it. Anyone with a public link can view and copy that page.

Your controls

You can export saved work, remove content using the available project and record controls, revoke published links and manage sign-in sessions. Clear this site's browser storage to remove device copies. You can revoke Google access in your Google Account connections. Signing out or revoking Google access does not delete saved COMPASS content.

Previous revisions and backups can retain earlier copies. To request access to or deletion of account data, contact info@aimedia.global. We may need to verify ownership before acting.

Voice input

Your browser handles speech recognition and may use its own speech service. COMPASS receives the text you submit.
