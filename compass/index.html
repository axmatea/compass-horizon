THE IDEA BEHIND COMPASS

Keep the work.
Keep the reason.

Work is more than a list of tasks. It is the people, the context, and the reasons that give the next step meaning.

PeopleStart with the peopleBehind every piece of work is a person, a perspective, and a reason to care.
ContextRemember the sourceThe original conversation matters as much as the conclusion it leads to.
PurposeKeep the reason closeA shared understanding of why gives direction to what comes next.
DirectionMove with intentionThe COMPASS idea is simple: less lost context, more meaningful work together.
