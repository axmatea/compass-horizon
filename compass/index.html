How it works, in eight beats

01 · INDIVIDUALS

Every person. Their own context.

Everyone works alone. The context lives in separate heads.

02 · CONNECT

Context finds context.

A call, a thread and a doc turn out to be about the same thing.

03 · SHARED MEMORY

Underneath the office, one memory.

Conversations, relationships, meetings, documents, decisions, tasks. Every item keeps its source.

04 · CHANGE

Then something changes.

The client moves the launch to October 3.

05 · ADAPT

It knows what depends on what.

Goal, owner, constraint, decision, next action. The plan updates, not the whole team.

GOAL
Launch on Oct 3 without losing the press window
OWNER
Ana
CONSTRAINT

DECISION

NEXT ACTION
Maya moves press to Oct 1 · Leo freezes Sep 29 · Ana confirms with the client today

06 · COORDINATE

Only the people it affects.

Only the people it touches hear about it. Everyone else keeps working.

07 · ACT

Everyone knows their next move.

Maya moves the press date. Leo re-scopes the build. Ana tells the client.

08 · MEMORY

Smarter every week.

Long histories compress into usable context. The reason behind every decision stays.
