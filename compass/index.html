        Questions

        What is COMPASS?

        COMPASS turns intent into real software. You describe what you need, by voice or text, and it builds web apps, mobile apps, websites and internal tools.

        What does the builder on this page do?

        It takes what you say or type, keeps a brief, and renders a prepared page for desktop and mobile. You can change the direction, copy the brief, and download the HTML preview. This early-access demo uses prepared examples while the AI provider is being confirmed. The page lives in your session and nothing is published.

        Do I need to know how to code?

        No. You describe the outcome in plain language and answer the questions COMPASS asks.

        How is it different from Lovable, Bolt, Replit or v0?

        Those begin with a typed prompt. COMPASS begins with a conversation: voice first, clarifying questions, and a web and phone preview you can change by talking.

        Is my voice recorded?

        Voice input uses your browser's speech recognition. COMPASS receives only the text, and you can always type instead.

        Who is building it?

        AX Media Global, a B2B SaaS studio with products for CPAs, developers and fintech teams.
