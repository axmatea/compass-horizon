COMPASS — Hackathon presentation
Tagline: Find your next step.
Positioning: A voice-first thinking partner designed to follow your changing thoughts and help you choose a clear next action.

3-minute presentation
0:00–0:20  The problem: we often need to talk before we know what we mean. Our first request is not always our real goal.
0:20–1:20  Play the complete 60-second film with sound.
1:20–2:20  Open Try the concept. Choose the cofounder story. Start with wanting to leave, change direction toward repairing the partnership, and show the revised context and next-action card.
2:20–2:45  Demonstrate editing or downloading the next step. Optional: use browser dictation if supported.
2:45–3:00  State the boundary clearly: this is a product concept with scripted responses, not a deployed open-ended AI system.

What works in this first version
- Finished 60-second, 1080p/24fps film with dialogue, narration and original music.
- Interactive guided scenarios, context-state revision and editable action cards.
- Optional browser speech recognition and browser text-to-speech, subject to support and permission.
- Responsive site, film captions and transcript, presentation mode.
- Downloadable original footage, music, reference frames and picture EDL for DaVinci Resolve.

Product vision, not implemented
- Open-ended AI reasoning, persistent memory and interruption-aware realtime dialogue.
- External integrations or autonomous actions.
- No claim of customer adoption, measured effectiveness or shipped capabilities.

Presentation setup
Use a modern desktop browser. Test speakers and load the film once before judging begins.
The film and the original footage are separate deliverables. The site does not require an account.
Use the guided choices for a reliable demo; microphone input is optional and may use your browser provider's speech service.
